Staying longer

For a week or more, the questions change. This is the list we would want on day two.

Working

  • In the studio: fast Wi-Fi, a desk, air conditioning, and a courtyard that stays quiet. Most guests never need more.
  • Coworking: WeWork, Národní 14 · 10 min. Five floors, day pass from about 850 CZK, meeting rooms, the usual WeWork everything. wework.com · Map
  • Coworking: Opero, Salvátorská 8, Old Town · 20 min. Quieter and more polished, day pass about 1,000 CZK, weekdays 8–20. opero.cz · Map
  • Cafés that tolerate laptops: mamacoffee upstairs (3 min) on weekday mornings; Kavárna Pražírna's cellar (10 min). Café Neustadt does not, and says so.
  • Printing: Copy General, Senovážné náměstí 26 · 17 min, open daily until 23:00.

Moving

  • Transit pass: a 30-day Prague pass is 550 CZK, cheaper than four 72-hour tickets, bought in the PID Lítačka app or e-shop (pidlitacka.cz) with an account; no physical card needed. 90 days is 1,480 CZK.
  • Bikes and scooters: Rekola (pink bikes), Nextbike and Lime are everywhere. With a 30-day pass in the Lítačka app, the first 15 minutes of each Rekola or Nextbike ride are free, several rides a day.
  • Gym: Form Factory I. P. Pavlova, Bělehradská 128 · 10 min. Large, Les Mills classes, Finnish sauna, day and week passes at reception. formfactory.cz
  • Running: down to Náplavka and along the river to Vyšehrad and back is a flat 6 km. Letná and Stromovka for hills and shade.
  • Swimming: Podolí (Podolská 74, tram 3 or 17, 15 min) has 50 m indoor and outdoor pools with a view of Vyšehrad.

Eating in

  • Delivery groceries: Rohlik.cz (English app, 90-minute slots, excellent) or Košík. Both deliver to the door.
  • Markets: Náplavka on Saturday 8–14 (15 min); Jiřák on náměstí Jiřího z Poděbrad Wed–Fri 8–18 and Sat 8–14 (metro A, or a 25-minute walk through Vinohrady), the better one for vegetables and the Wednesday evening crowd.
  • Butcher: Kantýna's counter (13 min) sells the dry-aged beef and sausages to take home. Cheese and wine: Vinograf's shop, Senovážné náměstí.
  • Recycling: yellow street bins for plastic, blue for paper, green for colored glass, white for clear. Beer bottles carry a small deposit; return them to the supermarket.

Health

  • Urgent: 112, or the emergency department at VFN, U Nemocnice 2 · 7 min, 24/7.
  • Pharmacy 24/7: Lékárna Palackého, Palackého 5 · 7 min. Dental emergency in the same building.
  • English-speaking GP, non-urgent: Canadian Medical (Dejvická) and Unicare (Bořislavka) are the established expat clinics, 20 minutes by metro A; both take same-week appointments and private payment. AMC (amcenters.com) takes walk-ins and does house calls. A current list.
  • Physio and massage: plenty on Vinohradská; ask us for a current name.

Everyday

  • Laundry: washer and dryer in the studio. Dry cleaning: look for "čistírna"; there are several within ten minutes.
  • Parcels: use a Zásilkovna or Alza box as the address rather than the building; there is no reception here.
  • SIM: Vodafone, O2 and T-Mobile on Wenceslas Square; a prepaid SIM is about 300–500 CZK with generous data. An eSIM from Airalo works before you land.
  • Haircut: walk-in barbers on Štěpánská and Vodičkova, typically 400–700 CZK.
  • Library: the Municipal Library on Mariánské náměstí (20 min) has a free reading room and the famous book tower at the entrance.
  • Quiet hours: 22:00–6:00, and the courtyard carries sound. Neighbors here are owners, not guests.

Sundays

Shops are open. Museums are open, Mondays are the closed day. Locals go to Vyšehrad, to the Vinohrady cafés, to Stromovka with the dog. Náplavka is calm. It is the best day of the week for the river walk to Vyšehrad and a long lunch at Čestr.
